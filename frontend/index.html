BEHIND THE RESULTS

Designed to find a better starting point.

Our search compares your request with book descriptions and topics. The default combines meaning-based matching with keyword matching, then orders the results using our own ranking rules.

This free demo searches a small sample of open-access books in your browser. It does not search NYU’s library catalog or the full 91,667-book research collection. The relevance number is relative to this search, not a probability that a book is right for you.
