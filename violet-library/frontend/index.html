    How search works

    Semantic search compares the meaning of your question with book descriptions and subjects. For a required title or phrase, put it in quotation marks. Select “Direct full text only” to skip catalog records.

    The collection is a sample of open-access books from DOAB, OpenStax, Project Gutenberg, Wikisource, universities, and public agencies. The language filter applies to the books, so you can ask in one language and read in another.
