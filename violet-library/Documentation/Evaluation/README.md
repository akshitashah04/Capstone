# Evaluation phase

This phase collects comparable results from our keyword, semantic, and hybrid search for the same 12 draft questions. The questions cover English, Spanish, French, and German, and include development and holdout groups. They are authored pilot prompts, not real student questions or evidence of relevance.

The [design](design.md) explains the measurements and their limits. The [review handoff design](review-design.md) explains the shared grading files. The [runbook](runbook.md) tells the team how to run the cloud job and judge results. The [session log](session-log.md) records what was built and verified. The [implementation plans](implementation-plan.md), [review plan](review-implementation-plan.md), and [diagnostic plan](answer-derived-plan.md) record the work sequence.

No book has a human relevance label yet. The collector leaves every grade blank, and the human-review scorer refuses to calculate relevance metrics until every returned question–book pair is judged. The separate [AI-judged diagnostic](answer-derived-results.md) uses its own 233-grade file and never fills the human sheets. Its scores describe the candidate pool, not confidence percentages.

The [first cloud pilot](https://github.com/VeedantBrahmbhatt/Libraries/actions/runs/43693270526) passed on 2026-11-15 and produced 233 distinct pairs for review. The [12 shared question sheets](../../evaluation/review/pilot/README.md) preserve the selected metadata in GitHub with blank grades. Its [original artifact](https://github.com/VeedantBrahmbhatt/Libraries/actions/runs/43693270526/artifacts/14153335931) is available until 2026-12-15. Neither source contains completed judgments or a relevance report.

The [review handoff cloud run](https://github.com/VeedantBrahmbhatt/Libraries/actions/runs/43694776677) verified that the saved sheets match a fresh search from the pinned index. The current human-review progress is **0 of 233 graded**. The [answer-derived cloud run](https://github.com/VeedantBrahmbhatt/Libraries/actions/runs/43775201104) tested 24 frozen questions against known source books. The [results report](answer-derived-results.md) compares all modes, explains the weak cases, and separates diagnostic numbers from official KPI assessment.

The later [50-persona study](persona-results.md) tests authored student-style requests from 25 undergraduates and 25 graduates. The [profiles and all 50 findings](../../evaluation/personas/README.md) are stored separately from human review. Hybrid found a clearly suitable book in the top five for 15/50, semantic for 14/50, and keyword for 11/50. Semantic placed a suitable book first more often. The study exposes reading-level and language gaps, but still uses fictional students and assistant judgments.

The subsequent [query-fit report](query-fit-results.md) and [runbook](query-fit-runbook.md) record the first five improvement steps: miss categories, source-provided metadata repair, explicit query-needs parsing, an experimental second ranking pass, and a separate textbook-coverage pilot. The v2 DOAB index is published, but no new ranking or supplement has passed human review. The second-pass model also misses the current warm-latency target.

The [v2 human-review design](human-review-v2-design.md), [plan](human-review-v2-plan.md), [runbook](human-review-v2-runbook.md), and [69 rank-blind sheets](../../evaluation/review/query-fit-v2/README.md) start the next phase. They freeze 723 DOAB candidate pairs from all four first-five rankings and 95 separate textbook candidates. All grades are blank. A lightweight GitHub check verifies edits and reports progress; quality metrics wait for complete genuine human grades. A separate natural-question holdout remains necessary before acceptance.

The existing 233-pair human-review sheets are pinned to **v1** for historical reproducibility. The new v2 candidate pool is stored separately; changing the old collector pin or copying its ratings into v2 would invalidate the comparison.

The [separate assistant v2 review](assistant-v2-results.md) judged the frozen 723 DOAB and 95 textbook pairs for a development KPI check while leaving every human grade blank. Hybrid found a clear fit in the first five for 17/50 authored questions; keyword found 10, semantic 14, and the slow second pass 15. The separate textbook search added two clear fits among 19 earlier misses. The [review plan](assistant-v2-review-plan.md), [labels and score reports](../../evaluation/ai-review/README.md), and per-question findings preserve the decisions and evidence. No method reached the requested more than 40/50 bar in this assistant diagnostic.

The [model comparison plan](embedding-model-comparison-plan.md), [runbook](embedding-model-comparison-runbook.md), and [final assistant results](embedding-model-comparison-results.md) tested E5 base and GTE with the same books and questions on temporary GitHub runners. First-five hybrid clear fits were **17/50** for current E5 small, **18/50** for E5 base, and **19/50** for GTE. The eight-shard GTE recovery run completed. Neither larger model meets the target, and no model is promoted before human review.

For a later meaningful assessment, collect 100–150 natural, anonymous student-style questions across the intended languages. The provisional targets are 80% of questions with a clearly useful book in the top five, hybrid top-ten quality at least 10% above keyword-only, 95% known-title success at rank one, and 95% of warm searches under two seconds. The small answer-derived pilot met the bars numerically, while the more varied 50-persona study fell well short of 80% top-five fit. Neither authored study establishes a pass for actual student use.
